If your non-profit organization manages a **Google Ad Grant** account ($10,000/month in free Search ads), maintaining strict policy compliance is mandatory.

A single missed policy violation can cause Google to suspend your $10,000/mo grant budget. Here is the step-by-step account audit checklist I use as a **Certified Google Partner** to keep non-profit grant accounts safe and performing at maximum efficiency.

## 1. The 5% CTR Minimum Rule

Google mandates that every Ad Grant account must maintain a minimum **5% Click-Through Rate (CTR)** at the account level each month.

* **How to Audit:** Check your account-level CTR for the last 30 days.
* **The Fix:** Pause low-performing keywords with high impressions but low clicks (e.g., broad general search terms).

## 2. Eliminate Disallowed Keywords

Google Ad Grant policy strictly forbids:
* **Single-word keywords** (e.g., searching just "charity" or "donate").
* **Overly generic keywords** (e.g., "free books", "help").
* **Keywords with a Quality Score of 1 or 2**.

* **The Fix:** Pause these keywords immediately or replace them with specific 2–3 word long-tail phrases.

## 3. Mandatory Conversion Tracking

Google requires all Ad Grants to have valid conversion tracking configured. You must receive at least 1 conversion per month, and your bidding strategy must focus on conversions rather than maximum clicks.

* **The Fix:** Ensure GA4 (Google Analytics 4) events are linked to Google Ads to track volunteer form submissions, newsletter signups, or donation button clicks.
